Course Project Details
Advanced NLP
Monsoon 2026

1

General instructions

Please read and follow these instructions throughout the project’s duration.
1. The project must be done in teams of 3-4.
2. You are highly encouraged to consult TAs during office hours to get inputs on project ideas.
3. You are highly encouraged to consult your team’s TA mentor for project related questions and guidance.
4. You are allowed to use AI and other internet resources for implementation and code. You are not allowed to
use AI to generate reports wherever relevant.
5. The project schedule and deadlines must be strictly followed. No extensions will be provided.
6. All submissions should follow the ACL Style Guide. LATEX files for this can be found here.
7. For each of the submissions, every team member must make the submission.

Project Proposal
Deadline 2.1 (Interim Proposal). 14th August 2026

Deadline 2.2 (Final Proposal). 28th August 2026

The interim submission should be a document outlining the project idea in about a page or two. This should
be treated similarly to a project abstract in that it should be a pitch of the project without being too short or
overcomplicated. It is natural that you would have a deeper idea of the project than what you submit, but the
submission should have enough information to give a fresh reader a sufficient idea of your project.
The filename for the interim proposal submission should be <TeamNameWithNoSpaces>-Interim.pdf
This proposal will be used to approve, revise, or reject your project idea. If the proposal is not accepted, you
will have a few days to revise and resubmit the final project proposal, which as the name suggests, will be the
project you will work on through the semester. You will also be assigned TA mentors who you’re expected to
consult throughout the course to obtain clarity on how to proceed with or rework the project.
The final proposal should be a more in-depth description containing the project idea, motivation, background,
related work, proposed methodology, dataset, and evaluation plan in 3-4 pages (excluding references). You may
also include a tentative timeline. You must include sections indicating the motivation behind the problem you’ve
selected, and the contributions you’ll be making. For clarity, the motivation is the shortcomings of existing work
that suggest the direction you choose, and the contribution is an elaboration on how your project addreses those
shortcomings (these will count for the page limit). You will be graded solely on the basis of the write-up.
The filename for the final proposal submission should be <TeamNameWithNoSpaces>-Proposal.pdf

Mid Submission
1

Deadline 3.1 (Mid Submission). 30th September 2026

This will be a document of 7-8 pages (excluding references) with an optional page for appendices. It should
cover a formal, refined statement of the problem, the progress you’ve made, and a concrete timeline of your plans
for the rest of the project. There should also be a comprehensive literature review, which should reflect the viability
and motivation for the project.
If you’ve used HuggingFace or WandB to train and upload models, include links to the relevant projects in the
README of the repository.
Additionally, the remote repository being used for your project should either be made public, or should grant
your TA mentors access to it. You will be graded on the basis of the write-up, code and a viva. Having slides for
the viva is optional.
The filename for the mid submission should be <TeamNameWithNoSpaces>-Mid.zip

4

Final Submission
Deadline 4.1 (Final Submission). 31st October 2026

This will be a document of 8 pages at most (excluding references) with an optional two pages of appendices.
This should be a complete write-up for your project structured like a research paper. All claims should be backed
up with either prior results or empirical results and sufficient analysis. Try to make all research claims as watertight
as possible. You may also include challenges, limitations and potential future work, which will not be considered
in the page count, as long as it is in a separate section just before the references. Make sure that this section
only contains this, and not any result from your project since any such result will be treated as future work or a
limitation.
If you’ve used HuggingFace or WandB to train and upload models, include links to the relevant projects in the
README of the repository.
You will be graded on the basis of all aspects of the submission: the write-up, the code (as a ZIP archive in the
submission) and the final viva which will require a presentation (preferably as a PDF in the submission).
The filename for the final submission should be <TeamNameWithNoSpaces>-Final.zip
