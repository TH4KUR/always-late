## Editorial rules

1. A fixed model/dictionary permits paired controlled comparisons; it does not establish a universal ontology of language circuits.
2. The 2B main study includes three simulated weight-quantization arms; the 9B extension is original RTN only, and Llama is a limited additional model/dictionary check.
3. Continuous activation correlation adds 3.1–4.4% held-out MAE improvement with positive intervals. Failure of a 10% convention is not evidence of zero information.
4. Ablation estimates dependence on an edit, not exclusive language representation or mediation of total quantization damage.
5. A missing extension bounds an answer's scope; it does not invalidate completed evidence. Real definition errors and adjustment-sensitive outcomes remain explicit.
6. Final synthesis questions are retrospective; they do not relabel exploratory results as preregistered confirmation.
