# Study G, G3: calibration of the ratios on the pilot split

Pilot split (100 sentences per language), K64 groups and 2 matched-control draws scaled with the same ratios; medians over the languages in nats per token. The rule (design section 3) was fixed before the runs: z = FP median net harm of zero-ablation; the three down ratios are the grid values whose FP net harm is nearest to 0.10 z, 0.30 z and 0.60 z; the up ratios are their reciprocals; the two fine up ratios (1.1, 1.2) are never chosen by the rule.

**Result: down ratios 0.3536, 0.1768, 0.0884; up ratios 2.8284, 5.6569, 11.3137 (branch `targets`; z = 0.2445).**
