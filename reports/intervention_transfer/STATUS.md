## 4. Choices to freeze (proposals written now, before any Q output exists; the freeze script will encode exactly these unless an amendment says otherwise)

- **T. Stored statistics, per feature, direction, context (sentence) and quantized state: the 5 x 5 Gram matrix of the centered-logit effect vectors of the five cells (FP/FP, Q/FP, FP/Q, Q/Q at FP amplitude, Q/Q at native amplitude), accumulated in float64 from float32 logits, plus the signed loss change of each cell, the number of edited positions, the number of positions where the feature is active in the state, and the native unedited hybrid loss of each cell.** The Gram gives every norm, cosine, error norm and the exact norms of C, W, I without storing 256,000-dimensional vectors (about 19 GB for the per-feature sums alone: 404 features x 5 directions x 9 cells x 256,000 floats, and 16 times that per context). Per feature the sum of the per-context vectors is accumulated on the GPU and its Gram is stored too, so the 'similarity of averaged fingerprints' of H4 is computed from the same forwards. A 'context' is one dev sentence; its vector is the mean over its edited positions (as F5 averages over positions).
- **F. Numerical floor and eligibility.** floor = the 95th percentile, over the calibration contexts (item S), of the norm of D[INT16] minus D[FP] at the same contexts, features and directions (the rounding-only state, same batch regime and readout); if that is exactly zero for a cell the repeated-batch pseudo-state of F5 is used instead. A context-feature pair is eligible if its FP effect norm exceeds 5 x floor. The normalized transfer error is ||D[Q,Q] - D[FP,FP]|| / (||D[FP,FP]|| + floor); unnormalized error, norm ratio, cosine and signed loss change are reported as well; counts and absolute outcomes of excluded pairs are reported. The floor is fixed from calibration contexts only; a test that recomputes it from held-out outcomes must fail.
- **S. Splits (all deterministic, by SHA-256 of a frozen key, fixed before outputs).** Feature level: the 404 features are split by the hash of the feature id into calibration (half) and held-out (half), stratified by language so that every language has both; document level: the FLORES source sentence id is the cluster (parallel translations share it), contexts of the calibration features and of the held-out features are never pooled in the fit; the ridge strength is chosen by feature-group folds inside the calibration half. Group level: the 27 x 300 evaluation sentences are used for the explanatory C, W, I contrasts (exploratory; devtest was examined before) and are NOT used to fit anything. External panel: 100 calibration and 300 evaluation passages per language, distinct documents.
- **X. External panel (six languages, 100 + 300 passages, at most 128 tokens).** `wikimedia/wikipedia` 20231101, revision above, first parquet shard of each of the six configs; passages of 64 to 128 tokens cut at sentence ends from distinct articles chosen by SHA-256 order of the article id; exact and whitespace/case-normalized duplicates of any FLORES line removed; **`zho_Hans`: a passage is kept only if it contains none of a fixed list of 50 high-frequency Traditional-only characters (the list is written into the freeze); if fewer than 400 pass, `zho_Hans` is recorded as missing, never replaced**. If the corpus cannot be fetched and frozen within the Task 1 time box (4 hours), external validation is recorded as missing and old-data results stay exploratory; no replacement after outcomes.
- **D. Directions.** Real decoder direction; the matched swapped direction (F5 rule); three random directions drawn with seeds 1, 2, 3, normalized to the real direction's norm and kept orthogonal to it: auxiliary calibration directions, not extra independent samples.
- **G. Mandatory gradient baseline (A2).** First-order prediction of the effect of the FP edit at each cell: the Jacobian-vector product of the centered logits of the suffix of state s with respect to the residual at the edited positions, along the edit (-a v), evaluated at the cached residual of prefix state p; the baseline predicts D[Q,Q] from the first-order effect at the native Q context and Q suffix, and is entered as a predictor in the nested comparison. Cost: one forward-mode pass per context; the Task 2 instrument gate measures it and, if it does not fit, the plan's rule applies (drop random-direction expansion first; the gradient baseline is not dropped).
- **Q. Endpoint and utility rule (spec).** Primary target: the normalized per-context transfer error above. Decision criterion for the diagnostic: at least 10 % relative held-out MAE reduction over the strongest prespecified baseline with a paired cluster interval excluding zero improvement, in Gemma-2-2B (A1). Nested ridge models A (activation correlation, log FP activation, firing rate, log residual norm), B (A plus matched/random-direction sensitivity measured with INDEPENDENT directions), and A plus the gradient baseline G; ridge strength from {0.1, 1, 10}, standardization fitted on calibration only; reported within the state, never pooled across INT4 and INT6.
- **H. Hypotheses (two-sided), as in the spec, plus A3.** H1 native effect error needs more than activation drift or a scalar change of perturbation size; H2 the crossed contrasts explain a reproducible part of the error and the interaction I is measured; H3 generic sensitivity predicts some transfer error and feature- or context-specific variation may remain; H4 per-context transfer differs from similarity of averaged fingerprints (same feature-context pairs); A3 |C| > |W|.
