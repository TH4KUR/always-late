# Novelty reassessment after D-094

Date: 26 October 2026. Post-result scoping review, not an exhaustive priority search.
This is the current novelty verdict; earlier memos, verification and source registers
remain historical records. This update changes documentation, not frozen analyses.

## Verdict

**We do not yet have an established A-tier contribution. The broad novelty claim must be withdrawn.**
Several ingredients are already demonstrated, and the original practical diagnostic failed
its overall utility criterion. Not finding an identical experiment does not make the result
substantial, establish priority, or justify describing it as “verified novel.”

The remaining candidate is a **controlled empirical evaluation of fixed-SAE intervention
preservation under weight quantization**, separating intervention definition, activation
background, downstream weights and measurement arithmetic, with independent validation and
a consequential outcome. Its exact completed combination was not found in this search.
That is a provisional scope distinction, not a publishable result already earned.

## What the actual results permit

The [main result tables](intervention_transfer/results/main/README.md) support measured
INT4 intervention drift under this instrument, one model/layer/SAE and simulated RTN.
They do not support selective language-feature destruction, restored comprehension, or a
general deployment-safety claim. INT16 RTN is a genuine small weight change, not a pure
arithmetic-noise oracle.

The original feature-held-out diagnostic improves MAE by 21.5% at INT4, 10.9% at INT6
and 23.4% at INT16. Overall utility is **not met**. About 88% of held-out-feature documents
also occur in calibration. The [post hoc audit](publication_reassessment_2026-10-26/README.md)
restricts the fixed fits to 136 eligible items on 65 unseen documents: gains are 6.73%,
0.46% and 29.4%, respectively. Those small-subset estimates have no new intervals;
they do not prove that the gain vanished. They make independent prediction essential.
Four costly control interventions on each test context also do not establish a cheap audit.

At group level, context dominance concerns the **fixed FP edit**. INT4 native net harm
(median .4325) is close to the weights-only cell (.4284), whereas the fixed edit gives
.8479. This does not establish equivalence, but it prevents transferring the fixed-edit
mechanism interpretation to native edits. Feature-vector norms and signed group loss
are different estimands. Pearson's limited conditional predictive utility is not universal
metric invalidity. A scalar loss-gradient baseline is not a full logit-vector JVP baseline.

The research opportunity is therefore an explanatory evaluation with a carefully bounded
answer, not a diagnostic-method or repair paper already established by these results.

## Future-work motivation and what remains unearned

- Duan v2, conclusion and section 7 (PDF p.11), explicitly calls for “causal validation as
  the next step,” including intervention behavior under quantization. This motivates our
  central question; it does not reserve it or certify our protocol.
- FEGA v1, section 8 (pp.21–22), proposes naturalistic generation and successive-layer
  propagation measurements. The layerwise branch is a compression extension of an existing
  proposal; it is deferred, not a new standalone discovery.
- Chang's peer-reviewed paper, section 9, motivates extrinsic downstream evaluation.
  Its broad invitation is not evidence that our particular behavioral experiment is new.

An explicitly proposed future direction has already been discussed. The useful novelty
question is whether it has been answered and what our answer adds. Demanding both an
explicit future-work statement and that nobody has discussed the direction is incompatible.

Keep the [recovery plan](../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-26-publication-recovery-after-results.md):
finish the frozen external analysis; score locked FLORES models on Wikipedia without
refitting; resolve output-scale/common-support controls and the gradient-target mismatch;
then perform the bounded arithmetic audit only if justified. External outcomes are pending
in the latest recorded ledger and were not used in this novelty reassessment.

For a strong paper, the result must change a substantive conclusion: for example, a
replicable boundary showing when activation survival misclassifies a behaviorally relevant
intervention after fair controls, or which apparent transfer failures disappear under
controlled arithmetic. These are conditional possibilities, not predictions or new success
criteria for the old study. A new protocol must be specified before new outcomes are read.
If the finding is generic sensitivity already seen in controls, weak external prediction,
or cosine drift without consequence, the evidence remains insufficient for the desired
A-tier claim. Preserve the nulls and finish the course report honestly.
