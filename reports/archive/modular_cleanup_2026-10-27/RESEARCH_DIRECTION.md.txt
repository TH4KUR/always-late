## Publication assessment

**The broad novelty claim is too strong. We do not yet have an established A-tier contribution.**
Context-dependent SAE effects, strength controls, interactions, steering versus model use,
and decision-margin failures all have direct precedents. No identical completed fixed-SAE
prefix/suffix quantization study was found in the bounded search; that does not establish
priority or sufficient significance. Read the [current novelty assessment](reports/novelty_assessment_2026-10-26.md).

The remaining candidate is a controlled empirical answer to: **which definitions of SAE
intervention preservation remain reliable under weight quantization, independent data and
measurement precision?** This question is motivated by Duan's explicit causal-validation
future work. The precision result and independent predictive utility remain unestablished.
