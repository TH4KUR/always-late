No new estimators: only the existing ones with different inputs.

Define, for every supported feature f that is evaluable at its peak language (full-precision firing rate there > 0.001): `m(f) = max_activation[peak(f), f]` (the full-precision stream of the same file; Duan's own predictor), and survival `y_b(f) = corr[b][peak(f), f] > 0.9` (strict), b in {7, 6} confirmatory widths, 5 descriptive.

- **N6a, threshold-free dose-response (no new thresholds).** Within each firing-rate stratum (saved `strata`), Spearman correlation between S and `y_b` (and between S and r); combine strata by n-weighted mean; two-sided permutation p by permuting S within strata (10,000 draws, seed 0). Also a table of stratum-standardised survival by S decile (deciles of S over the evaluable supported features; standardise each decile's within-stratum rates to the overall stratum shares; report cells dropped). This is the proposal's continuous S~ moderator without a cut.
- **N6b, descriptive magnitude check.** Median `m` of selective vs controls at tau in {null, 3, 5} (using `phase1_threshold.alternative_selection`); within-stratum Spearman(S, m) and Spearman(m, `y_b`) (does magnitude predict survival in our data as in Duan's?).
