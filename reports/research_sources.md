# Research sources discussed during the decision review

Updated: 2026-10-21. This is the source register for the Q1-Q4 and Q13-Q15 discussions, including papers, official model/data/code sources and research-practice guidance. It supplements the [method reassessment](survival_method_reassessment.md) and [analysis log](progress_log.md); it is not the team's paper bibliography. Existing `paper/` files are not edited by this review.

## How to use and extend this register

Keep one entry per work and add later publication/version information to that entry. Record the official venue or source, the section inspected, the claim supported, its limits and the decision it informed. An arXiv upload is not verification of conference acceptance. Workshop publication, Findings, main-conference publication and a technical report are labelled separately. Publication establishes review/publication status, not guaranteed correctness or independent replication. A preprint can overlap a proposed contribution even when it cannot meet the user's requirement for verified published evidence.

Sources P01-P05 and P17-P18 were already discussed or checked earlier in this chat/project review; their verification limits are carried forward. P06-P16 were checked during the 2026-10-21 literature work. This register indexes sources used in the discussion, not every search-engine hit.

## Q13-Q15 synthesis adopted on 2026-10-21

**Q13 (D-032).** General activation-dependent quantization is already explored (P10/P11; P13 is a limited language case study). The specific causal role of baseline magnitude in our language-selective-feature comparison was not settled by the verified publications found. Report both matching comparisons as associations. Investigate whether language specificity predicts vulnerability beyond baseline magnitude and whether a justified causal design can distinguish a mechanism from selection/measurement effects. This is an approved question, not a novelty certificate. Baseline magnitude predates quantization; it can indicate susceptibility, but cannot mediate that subsequent intervention. A mediation claim about training/language representation would need a different, explicit causal pathway (P12).

**Q15 (D-034).** The user's hypothesis is retained, not adopted as a finding: lower predictive confidence -> more diffuse activity across more, weaker features, affecting features broadly. Test confidence, active-feature count, concentration and magnitude separately. Existing N9 supports lower aggregate scale over active features, not lower magnitude for every feature, more active features, or a confidence explanation. Thresholding can reduce the active count when responses weaken. P03 motivates a competing SAE-fidelity explanation; P15 separates confidence from accuracy; P16 shows uncertainty need not weaken every feature. P17 is close prior art with unverified publication status. The approved next design begins with available artifacts and compares these alternatives, including residual/pre-post-normalization scale and normalized SAE reconstruction error, across mask INT8/7/6/5/4. No exact GPU job, cost, metric threshold or new corpus is selected by this decision.
