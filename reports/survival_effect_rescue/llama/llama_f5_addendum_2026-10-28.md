## 0. What this check is, and is not

A **portability check** of the Gemma Stage 3 question on a second model-plus-SAE setup: when the continuous activation correlation R1 of a feature is higher, is the effect similarity W1 of its single-feature ablation higher, beyond simple baselines? Model, SAE family, SAE training data, tokenizer, size and layer depth all differ from Gemma (Gemma-2-2B layer 12 of 26; Llama layer 12 of 16, hook `blocks.12.hook_resid_pre` = `blocks.11.hook_resid_post`), so a difference cannot be attributed to any one of them. **Feature identities are never compared or matched across the two SAEs**; Llama features are selected from Llama's own development split. The conclusion concerns these two setups only; with two fixed systems no population claim is made and a non-significant difference is not equivalence. Llama's earlier group-level check was "not consistent" with Gemma (D-090, median group effect 0.048 nats per token at full precision, pre-registered P1 not met), so noise-dominated fingerprints are a real risk; gate 5 below is the guard.

## 2. Gates, in order (a failed gate ends the check and the Gemma result stands)

1. The model and SAE load and reproduce the frozen configuration (hook alias, k = 48, 14,336 features, d_model 2048); the smoke job (45215) completes with its logit gates (top-1 agreement at least 0.95, largest difference at most 2.0, native and float32) and the in-run sham gate exact.
2. Timing and memory: the measured per-state time of the smoke job, scaled to 643 features, fits the remaining schedule (the full job must be submitted by the evening of 29 October 2026 to leave time for validation and analysis before the 31 October deliverables) and 11 GB.
3. Disk and the two-job rule hold.
4. The full job passes the same validation as the Gemma F5 jobs (complete meta, gates true, state list FP 16 6 4, variant flags off, sham gate exact, files copied with sha256 equal to Ada's).
5. **Noise check (Llama specific):** the instrument is adequate if the median W1 between FP and the INT16 repeat is at least 0.7 (`ADEQUATE_MEDIAN`, the F5 design rule); the reliable-fingerprint population is defined with Llama's OWN floor (the 5th percentile of the INT16 cosine over its features), not Gemma's 0.9489. If the median INT16 cosine is below 0.7, or fewer than 300 features are reliable, the analysis stops with "instrument inadequate" and no relation is reported.

## 3. Analysis (identical to the Gemma Stage 3 freeze except where stated)

Unit: the feature; clusters: the 27 languages. Cells: INT6 and INT4 (two cells). Primary population: reliable fingerprints (INT16 cosine at least Llama's floor); noisy-effect stratum and the two sensitivity populations (S-all; S-loss with |dnll_FP| at least the 95th percentile of |dnll_INT16 - dnll_FP|) exactly as in the Gemma freeze. Predictors and baselines as in the Gemma freeze, with these substitutions fixed here: the native firing rate and mean activation of a feature are the values in the sample file (`native.FP`, from the Llama dev tagging stream, the counterpart of the Phase 1 values used for Gemma), `n_active` from stage A, |dnll| and the number of ablated positions from the FP state. Models A, B, C, null; rank-quantile predictors; leave-one-language-out ridge with the same lambda grid and grouped inner cross-validation; improvement = 1 - MAE_B / MAE_A; language-cluster bootstrap, 2,000 replicates, seed 20261028; Spearman and partial Spearman given the baselines; the same decision rule per cell (improvement at least 10 % with the lower end of the interval above 0). Overall for Llama: "adds predictive value" if both cells meet it, "mixed" if one, "no clear predictive value beyond baselines" if none. The reproduction control of the Gemma code (committed values reproduced) already passed; for Llama the control is the Gemma pipeline's unchanged functions plus the F5 analysis' own floor and adequacy check on the Llama files.

**Side by side with Gemma (legacy RTN cells, same states, same rule):** for each of INT6 and INT4 the Spearman correlation, the partial Spearman, the improvement of B over A with intervals, the system's own noise floor and the number of reliable features, in one table. Labels, defined now: the association **repeats** if the partial Spearman interval of the Llama cell lies above 0 (as it does for every Gemma cell); it **cannot be resolved** if that interval contains 0; it **reverses** if it lies below 0; predictive value **repeats** only if the Llama cell meets the 10 % rule, which no Gemma cell did. No pooled estimate across the two systems; no claim of equivalence from overlapping intervals.

## 4. Stop rules and what is not claimed

Any failed gate: stop, report the reason, finish with the Gemma result. No change of the sample, the states, the cutoffs or the models after seeing Llama outcomes; no steering, no GPTQ on Llama, no other model. Not claimed: anything about why the two systems differ; feature-level correspondence; a population of models; document-level generalization (no read-side document ids); causal reading (R1 and W1 share the perturbed model).
